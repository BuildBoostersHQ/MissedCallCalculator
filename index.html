Missed Call Cost Calculator

A free calculator that shows a contractor what unanswered calls cost in a year, using the missed-call math: missed calls per week × 52 × close rate × average job. It's open source and you can embed it on any website. Built by Build Boosters.

Embed it on your site

<div data-missed-call-calculator></div>
<script src="https://buildboostershq.github.io/MissedCallCalculator/embed.js" async></script>

Optional settings go on the div: data-calls="3", data-job="1500", data-close="20", data-theme="light" and data-accent="#f2a20c".
